# MUDRA — Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

source_url: https://www.mudra.org.in/

## Overview

MUDRA (Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency) provides collateral-free loans to non-farm micro and small enterprises through member lending institutions (banks, MFIs, NBFCs).

## Loan Tiers

MUDRA loans are classified into four tiers based on the loan amount:

- **Shishu:** Up to ₹50,000 — for early-stage startups needing seed funding.
- **Kishore:** ₹50,001 to ₹5,00,000 — for established micro units needing growth capital.
- **Tarun:** ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 — for mature units with a track record.
- **Tarun Plus:** ₹10,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 — for units that have repaid a prior Tarun loan.

## Eligibility

- Any Indian citizen aged 18 or above.
- Engaged in income-generating activity in manufacturing, trading, or services.
- Both new and existing businesses are eligible.
- No minimum education requirement.
- Tarun Plus requires that a previous Tarun MUDRA loan has been fully repaid.

## Interest Rate and Repayment

- Interest rate is set by the lending institution; typical rates range from 8.5% to 12% per annum.
- Tenure: up to 5 years (60 months).
- Moratorium: up to 6 months (interest serviced during moratorium).
- No collateral or processing fee for loans up to ₹10 lakh (as per RBI guidelines).

## Subsidy

No capital subsidy under MUDRA. Interest subvention available for select categories under separate schemes (not part of core MUDRA terms).

## How to Apply

Apply through any MUDRA-member bank, Regional Rural Bank, MFI, or NBFC. Documents required: Aadhaar, PAN, business plan, bank statement.
