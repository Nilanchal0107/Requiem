# KCC — Kisan Credit Card (Modified Interest Subvention Scheme)

source_url: https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2131989

## Overview

The Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS) is a Central Sector Scheme that ensures short-term credit reaches farmers at an affordable interest rate through the Kisan Credit Card (KCC). The Union Cabinet approved its continuation for FY 2025-26 on 28 May 2025 with the existing 1.5% interest subvention. No change was proposed in the structure or other components of the scheme.

## Loan Limit and Interest Rate

- Farmers receive short-term loans of up to ₹3 lakh through Kisan Credit Cards at a subsidised interest rate of 7%.
- A 1.5% interest subvention is provided to eligible lending institutions.

## Prompt Repayment Incentive

Farmers who repay promptly are eligible for an incentive of up to 3% (Prompt Repayment Incentive), which effectively reduces their interest rate on KCC loans to 4%.

## Animal Husbandry and Fisheries

For loans taken exclusively for animal husbandry or fisheries, the interest benefit applies up to ₹2 lakh.
