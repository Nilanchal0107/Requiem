# Hyper Local Business & Loan Advisory

Helps a first-time rural entrepreneur decide whether a business will work in their locality, what it costs, and which government loan scheme can fund it.

## Language

### People and places

**Entrepreneur**:
The person evaluating a business idea for their own locality.
_Avoid_: User, customer, applicant, farmer

**Locality**:
The village (with its block and district) the Entrepreneur plans to operate in.
_Avoid_: Location, area, region

### Business

**Business Archetype**:
A business type in the catalogue that has a curated cost template, revenue model and demand assumptions (dairy, tailoring, kirana, poultry, flour mill, onion cultivation).
_Avoid_: Business idea, category, template

**Comparison**:
Two or three Business Archetypes evaluated side by side for the same Entrepreneur, Locality and Own Contribution.
_Avoid_: Benchmark, versus

**Project Cost**:
The total one-time investment needed to start the business.
_Avoid_: Investment, capex, startup cost

**Own Contribution**:
The money the Entrepreneur puts into the project from their own funds.
_Avoid_: Available capital, margin, savings

**Aggregator**:
A buyer that takes all of a business's output regardless of local household demand, such as a cooperative milk collection centre or an APMC mandi.
_Avoid_: Buyer, market yard, middleman

**Market Gap**:
Estimated local demand minus the capacity of existing competitors in the Locality.
_Avoid_: Opportunity, unmet demand

**Source Badge**:
The label on every number saying where it came from: Census, OSM, User-entered or Assumption.
_Avoid_: Confidence tag, data label

**Funding Gap**:
Project Cost minus Own Contribution: the amount that must come from a loan, before any subsidy.
_Avoid_: Loan requirement, shortfall

### Finance

**Scheme**:
A government loan or subsidy programme with fixed eligibility rules and terms (e.g. MUDRA, PMEGP).
_Avoid_: Loan, yojana, programme

**Verdict**:
The rule-based outcome for a DPR: Feasible, Feasible with changes, or Not feasible.
_Avoid_: Score, rating, assessment

**Path to Yes**:
The smallest changes (more Own Contribution, smaller unit scale) that move a Verdict up to Feasible.
_Avoid_: Recommendations, suggestions, fixes

**DPR**:
Detailed Project Report: the single source of truth built from the Entrepreneur's inputs, market analysis and matched Scheme, shown as a Bank View and an Entrepreneur View.
_Avoid_: Feasibility report, project report, business plan

**Bank View**:
The DPR in bank format (cost breakdown, means of finance, projections, repayment schedule), downloadable as a PDF.
_Avoid_: Report PDF, formal report

**Entrepreneur View**:
A plain-language explanation of the same DPR in the Entrepreneur's chosen language, with the verdict, SWOT, risks and next steps.
_Avoid_: Summary, AI report, explainer

**Verified Figure**:
A number in the Entrepreneur View that was supplied by the calculation engine rather than written by the AI, and links to its formula and source.
_Avoid_: Checked number, proof
