# PMEGP — Prime Minister Employment Generation Programme

source_url: https://www.kviconline.gov.in/pmegpeportal/

## Overview

PMEGP is a credit-linked subsidy programme administered by KVIC, Khadi Boards and DICs. It provides bank loans with a government subsidy to set up new micro enterprises in manufacturing, service and allied sectors.

## Eligible Activities

- Manufacturing: all sectors except those on the negative list.
- Service: rural and urban service enterprises.
- Allied: agro-processing, dairy-based, animal husbandry (non-farm activities).

## Project Cost Limits

- Manufacturing: up to ₹50 lakh.
- Service and retail: up to ₹20 lakh.

## Subsidy Rates (Capital, Back-Ended)

The subsidy is released to the bank after 3 years (lock-in period of 36 months) and applied against the outstanding principal. It does **not** reduce the EMI during the lock-in period.

| Category | Urban | Rural |
|---|---|---|
| General | 15% | 25% |
| SC/ST/OBC/Women/Ex-Servicemen/Minority/PH | 25% | 35% |

## Own Contribution

- General category: 10% of project cost.
- Special category (SC/ST/OBC/Women/Ex-Servicemen/Minority/PH): 5% of project cost.

## Eligibility

- Age: 18 years and above.
- New units only — existing businesses are not eligible.
- No prior government subsidy for the same unit.
- Education: 8th pass required for projects above ₹5 lakh (service/retail).

## Loan Terms

- Maximum loan: ₹47.5 lakh (i.e., project cost minus own contribution, for manufacturing ₹50 lakh unit).
- Interest rate: set by the lending bank; typically 10–12% per annum.
- Tenure: up to 7 years (84 months).
- Moratorium: up to 6 months.

## How to Apply

Apply online at kviconline.gov.in or through any nationalised bank, scheduled commercial bank or cooperative bank. EDP (Entrepreneurship Development Programme) training is mandatory before disbursement.
