# PM Vishwakarma

source_url: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in/

## Overview

PM Vishwakarma supports traditional artisans and craftspeople. Tailor (Darzi) is one of the eligible trades on the official list.

## Credit Support

- Collateral-free Enterprise Development Loans come in two tranches. The first tranche is ₹1 lakh, repayable over 18 months. The second tranche is ₹2 lakh, repayable over 30 months.
- The beneficiary pays a concessional interest rate of 5%. The Ministry of MSME pays an interest subvention capped at 8%.
- Credit guarantee fees are borne by the Government of India.

## Toolkit Incentive

A grant of ₹15,000 for a toolkit.

## Eligibility

- The applicant must be at least 18 years old on the registration date.
- Registration and benefits are limited to one member per family. Family means the husband, wife and unmarried children.
- The applicant must not have taken a loan under a similar Central or State credit scheme for self-employment or business development (for example PMEGP, PM SVANidhi or Mudra) in the past 5 years.
