# CMEGP — Maharashtra Chief Minister Employment Generation Programme

source_url: https://bankofmaharashtra.bank.in/cmegp

## Overview

CMEGP is a Maharashtra state programme that helps new micro enterprises through a bank term loan with a back-ended state subsidy.

## Eligibility

- Age 18 to 45 years, with a 5-year relaxation for special category applicants.
- Maharashtra resident. Applicants born outside the state need a domicile certificate.
- No income ceiling.
- New projects only. Existing units and units that have already received a government subsidy are not eligible.
- One person per family, where family means self and spouse.
- Proprietorship, partnership or registered Self-Help Groups.

## Project Cost Limit

Manufacturing projects: up to ₹50 lakh.

## Beneficiary Contribution and Subsidy (% of project cost)

| Category | Contribution | Subsidy (Urban) | Subsidy (Rural) |
|---|---|---|---|
| General | 10% | 15% | 25% |
| Special (SC/ST, women, ex-servicemen, differently abled) | 5% | 25% | 35% |

The bank provides the balance as a term loan: 90% of project cost for the general category and 95% for the special category.

## Loan Terms

- Repayment over 3 to 7 years after an initial moratorium set by the bank.
- The scheme text names no CMEGP-specific interest rate: the normal prevailing rate is charged.
- The margin money is held as a Term Deposit Receipt for three years (lock-in).

## Negative List

Meat processing, slaughtering or canning, beedi, pan, cigar or cigarette production or sale, dhabas or outlets serving liquor, tobacco preparation, toddy tapping, cultivation of tea, coffee or rubber, sericulture, horticulture and floriculture, animal husbandry including goat or sheep rearing, piggery and poultry, plastic, polythene and thermocol products, and any product or activity banned by the Government of India or the State Government.
