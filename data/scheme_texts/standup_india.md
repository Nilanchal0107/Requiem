# Stand-Up India — Scheme for Financing SC/ST and Women Entrepreneurs

source_url: https://www.standupmitra.in/Home/SumiSchemes

## Overview

Stand-Up India facilitates bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore to at least one SC or ST borrower and at least one woman borrower per bank branch for setting up a greenfield enterprise.

## Eligible Borrowers

- Women entrepreneurs (any category).
- SC (Scheduled Caste) entrepreneurs — male or female.
- ST (Scheduled Tribe) entrepreneurs — male or female.
- Age: 18 years and above.

## Eligible Enterprises

- Greenfield (new) enterprises only — no existing businesses.
- Sectors: manufacturing, service, and allied activities (e.g., agro-processing).

## Project Cost

- Minimum: ₹10 lakh.
- Maximum: ₹1 crore.

## Loan Terms

- Loan amount: 75% of project cost (i.e., 10% own contribution and remaining from promoter's contribution plus subsidy if any).
- Own contribution: minimum 10% of project cost.
- Interest rate: Base Rate (MCLR) + 3% + tenor premium; typically 10–11% per annum (assumption).
- Tenure: up to 7 years (84 months).
- Moratorium: up to 18 months on principal repayment.

## Subsidy

No direct capital subsidy under Stand-Up India. Convergence with PMEGP or state schemes is possible, but not automatic.

## Composite Loan

Stand-Up India provides a composite loan covering term loan (for fixed assets) and working capital. It is processed as a single facility.

## How to Apply

Apply through the Stand-Up Mitra portal (standupmitra.in) or contact any scheduled commercial bank branch. SIDBI, NABARD and DIC facilitate the process.
